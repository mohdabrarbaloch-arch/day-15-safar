About Safar

Safar runs a real kinematic simulation of a vehicle on a track and then reports the safety envelope of what happened. Every number on the dashboard is traceable to the timestep where it peaked - not a summary of a summary.

What it actually models

  • Kinematic bicycle model - position, heading, speed and steering, integrated with the midpoint rule.
  • Pure-pursuit path following with a speed-dependent lookahead, so it does not cut corners at speed.
  • IDM car-following for NPC traffic - free flow and congestion from the same three lines of maths.
  • Weather as friction - wet tarmac costs 38% of braking authority, and the stopping distances change accordingly.
  • Tabular Q-learning - 140 states, 9 actions, trained in-process and fully readable as a table.

What it is not

  • Not a photorealistic renderer. It is a 2D top-down canvas view.
  • Not a vehicle dynamics package. No tyre slip, no suspension, no load transfer.
  • Not a safety certification. The composite score is a documented heuristic.
  • Not a trained AV stack. The agent is deliberately small so its policy stays legible.
